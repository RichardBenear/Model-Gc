# EX-GC30A — Analytical screen of emergent common geometry (EXPLORATORY)

The analytical screen identifies multicomponent analogue media as the most tractable next falsification experiment, with collective tetrads as the stronger longer-term tensor candidate. Equal limiting speeds in one vacuum do not establish equivalence: an exactly exchange-symmetric two-component medium can have two distinct light cones. A common metric must be a robust output of microscopic dynamics across probe types and background deformations; none of the five arms yet meets the complete gravity criteria. The Sakharov mechanism remains relevant downstream as a possible origin of metric stiffness, not as a derivation of a universal metric.

## 1. No-go boundary

Weinberg–Witten [1] prohibits an energy-carrying massless helicity >1 particle in a Lorentz-invariant theory with an appropriate conserved Lorentz-covariant stress tensor representing total four-momentum. Not a blanket ban on emergent geometry. Ordinary GR evades the hypothesis concerning a gauge-invariant covariant local total gravitational stress tensor. Three candidate exits: microscopic Lorentz symmetry absent (A/B); genuinely emergent spacetime with no ordinary background-local stress tensor (D/E, only if actually realized); gauge redundancy/diffeomorphism constraints so the required tensor does not exist (possible future B/C completion, not demonstrated). A background metric alone is not an escape or a derivation. Weinberg's soft spin-2 argument [2] is a complementary conditional universality constraint under relativistic S-matrix/gauge consistency assumptions; it does not create the massless pole. Marolf [11] also exposes a locality/boundary-Hamiltonian obstacle to fully nonlinear universal energy coupling. Need explicitly identify microscopic hypotheses and show effective symmetry, not assert escape by vocabulary.

## 2. Exact analytical benchmark: two interacting condensate phase modes

Take Galilean microscopic Hamiltonian (hbar=1) H = integral sum_a [|grad psi_a|^2/(2m_a) + V_a n_a] + U(n_1,n_2), with psi_a=sqrt(n_a) exp(i theta_a). No curved metric supplied. At a stable homogeneous state define H_ab = partial_a partial_b U (positive definite; here H is a Hessian, not Hamiltonian), D_ab=delta_ab n_a/m_a, ignoring density gradient/quantum pressure at wavelengths much larger than healing lengths. For equal background velocity v, D_t=partial_t+v dot grad. Quadratic L = -delta_n^T D_t theta - (1/2)delta_n^T H delta_n - (1/2)sum_i partial_i theta^T D partial_i theta. Eliminating delta_n gives L=(1/2)(D_t theta)^T H^{-1}(D_t theta)-(1/2)sum_i partial_i theta^T D partial_i theta. Local principal symbol P=H^{-1}(omega-v dot k)^2-D |k|^2. Real frequencies and positive kinetic energy follow H,D>0. Normalize by H^{1/2}: determinant factors into two branches, (omega-v dot k)^2=c_j^2 |k|^2, c_j^2=eigenvalues(H^{1/2} D H^{1/2}). Each is a Lorentzian acoustic cone, with inverse metric proportional to matrix [-1,-v^T; -v,c_j^2 I-v v^T] in coordinates t,x. This derives kinematics, not gravity. Spatially varying backgrounds require WKB and common velocity; independent velocities are an additional splitting mechanism.

Exact common cone iff H^{1/2} D H^{1/2}=c^2 I, equivalently D=c^2 H^{-1}. For diagonal D this requires H12=0 in this simplified no-entrainment model; density mixing cannot be removed by rescaling species. Counterexample with exact exchange symmetry n1=n2=n, m1=m2=m, U=(g/2)(n1^2+n2^2)+g12 n1 n2, |g12|<g. Symmetric/antisymmetric modes c_+^2=(n/m)(g+g12), c_-^2=(n/m)(g-g12). Exchange symmetry allows g12. At g12/g=.1, in units c0=sqrt(ng/m), speeds sqrt(1.1)=1.04881 and sqrt(.9)=.948683, ratio 1.10554 (10.55% relative split). At g12=0 they coincide; infinitesimal exchange-preserving interaction spoils equality. This is a stable, explicit F for C3 in generic A. For uncoupled equal components a common flow/density deformation preserves equality only while the component backgrounds remain equal; independent perturbation breaks it. Standard single-fluid acoustic derivation [3] and two-component massive/massless tuned benchmark [4] agree with this logic; the latter has coherent mixing and is not described by the simplified Hessian formula at arbitrary gaps.

General local quadratic principal symbol P_ab(q) = K_ab^{mu nu} q_mu q_nu. Sufficient condition for common metric cone is K_ab^{mu nu}=Z_ab G^{mu nu}, Z positive (up to overall convention), modulo redundant terms. Not a necessary theorem for all gauge/constrained systems; use physical polarizations there. In a common-flow rest frame the SPD temporal tensor A and all spatial tensors B^{ij} must satisfy A^{-1/2} B^{ij} A^{-1/2}=b^{ij} I, similarly mixed coefficients. This catches off-diagonal strain, not just isotropic speed. Degenerate eigenvalues of a real symmetric 2x2 matrix force it to be proportional to identity. Exchange permits I and sigma_x for each coefficient, so independent ratios remain. An unbroken irreducible internal O(N) multiplet forces symmetric two-index coefficients to identity in species indices if every background is a singlet, but cannot protect equality between inequivalent reps or on a symmetry-breaking background. A single microscopic kinetic rule shared by symmetry-equivalent modes is a legitimate conditional kinematic output, not a result for all matter. A mass term distinguishing species can break that protection; radiative corrections require separate analysis.

Cone equality fixes a conformal class only. Massive probes may obey E^2=c^2 p^2+Delta^2 and respond to local gap and wavefunction normalization differently. Example constant cone g_a=Omega_a(x)^2 eta: species dependent rest energy m_a Omega_a in common coordinates implies distinct clock/free-fall behavior if Omega_a gradients differ. Do not infer a full common metric from speed equality. Need independent massive clocks and soliton tests in the same background.

A: Standard acoustic metric is a derived collective propagation geometry but restricted to a medium sector with microscopic rest frame; Weinberg–Witten exact microscopic Lorentz invariance absent. No massless helicity-2 in simple phase-density model. BEC backreaction can produce a short-range modified Poisson equation [5], a limitation rather than a gravitational mechanism. Sindoni's symmetric multi-BEC [6] has a long-range potential and weaker equivalence within multiplets; the singlet and gapped multiplets still require speed tuning. Relativistic condensate example [7] derives Nordstrom-type conformal gravity with massive and massless excitations in a special limit: important positive scalar precedent, not a tensor escape. Do not redo GC26 scalar gravity as though new.

B: A micro Hamiltonian near Weyl node a may be expanded H_a(p)=w_a^i delta_p_i I+sigma^j e_{aj}^i delta_p_i. Its determinant yields (omega-w_a dot delta_p)^2=delta_p^T e_a^T e_a delta_p. Nondegenerate e gives a Lorentzian cone, with tilt and anisotropy as outputs. Momentum-space topology protects a node under appropriate perturbations, not values of w or e. Two nodes can have different velocities/strain couplings even if both topologically protected; e_a^T e_a must coincide and tilts match. A microscopic lattice/medium lacks exact boost symmetry, so theorem hypothesis absent; no implication of a massless physical tensor fluctuation. Volovik/Zubkov [8] motivate composite vierbein fluctuations; collective elasticity commonly includes extra modes and preferred frame. Derive a shared tetrad from a specified microscopic Hamiltonian across inequivalent scalar, fermion and gauge probes before computing induced curvature action. Particularly interesting since our real-space soliton topology and momentum-space topology need not be the same thing.

C: Sakharov [9] gives quantum-vacuum contribution to curvature stiffness on specified geometry. For W[g]= +/- (1/2) Tr log D[g], a covariant derivative expansion contains vacuum volume, curvature and higher curvature terms; 28D already illustrates mass/regulator/coupling dependence. If every matter sector sees g, variation gives stress sourcing by definition. If instead different sectors have g_a, sum W_a[g_a] does not imply a shared g or eliminate relative tensor modes. An independently dynamical common metric and gauge redundancy would evade the local total stress-tensor assumption, but the induced loop computation itself supplies neither; background-field version no emergent spin-2 test. Reserve until propagation geometry is justified.

D: Jacobson [10] relates vacuum entanglement stationarity in small geodesic balls at fixed volume to semiclassical Einstein dynamics for special matter/variation assumptions. Holographic entanglement arguments [12] use boundary quantum states and a gravitational dual. Neither derives common geometry for arbitrary microscopic particle sectors in Gc. A geometry-free quantum Hilbert space need not possess the theorem's Lorentz-covariant local stress tensor, but whether the desired dynamical gauge symmetry emerges is open. Counterexample: two independent Gaussian quantum sectors on a lattice, H2 scaled by lambda>0, have the same ground state (and all equal-time entanglement/correlations) as before, while excitation energies and wave speeds of H2 scale by lambda. Thus static entanglement alone cannot fix a common clock or cone. Need unequal-time commutators, modular/real-time evolution and independently reconstructed geometry, not just equal-time correlation distances. No superluminal communication premise.

E: Causal set [13] begins with locally finite partial order, not local fields on a given spacetime. For a manifoldlike order, causal order constrains conformal geometry and event count supplies volume scale; no guarantee a generic graph is manifoldlike. Causal order is an input in that construction, not derived from arbitrary graph dynamics. Candidate escapes microscopic background-local Lorentz covariant stress tensor assumption if genuinely pregeometric. For Model Gc/CRR style relational models, measure independent signal propagation and interval volumes/dimension without inserting embedding. Shared adjacency alone does not force equal operational speed: two graph walkers can use distinct transition amplitudes on the same edges, even diffusion rather than hyperbolic propagation. No massless spin-2 identified. Keep as long-term alternative rather than deflect into graph census.

## 4. Selected next test (proposal only; no GC30B execution)

A is the lowest-cost discriminant and B is the main tensor reserve. GC30B should use a two-component BEC linear/Bogoliubov reference Hamiltonian with g, g12, masses, equilibrium density, coherent exchange lambda as in [4,6], plus controlled background flow/strain. Start with exact decoupled positive control and exchange-permitted negative control above, then reproduce literature tuned gapped/gapless case. Fix microscopic couplings once at homogeneous calibration; vary common & relative backgrounds, shared flow, anisotropy/strain (including off-diagonal components via physical hopping/kinetic terms), symmetry-preserving interaction and gap; require stable real spectrum and fit dispersion in multiple directions and flow perturbations, with finite-k/healing-length checks. Extract temporal/spatial/mixed principal coefficient matrices separately, compare canonically normalized tensors, not a fit to a preselected metric. Two branches must be independently identified (eigenvectors, gaps); record mode mixing, preferred-frame corrections (quartic k), single-sector control. Distinguish symmetry-preserving parameter perturbation from deliberately broken symmetry. Exact analytic control establishes expected degeneracy failure; numerical test only needed for full mixed gapped and inhomogeneous response. Include at least one genuinely different mode (massive vs massless); if only same multiplet passes label conditional. Propose in GC30B a predeclared tolerance from convergence error (e.g. max 1% cone mismatch with <0.2% numerical/fit uncertainty), plus open parameter neighborhood and no re-fit; exact symmetry argument still required for general universality. Massive dispersion normalization and local gaps must independently predict clock/force response before C4 pass; wavecones not enough. If tuned only, stop and move to B (derive node response under strain or propose nontrivial common microscopic kinetic symmetry); do not run GC30C on a failed common-geometry candidate. If positive, only a kinematic candidate, then test energy sourcing including binding/excitation/radiation and whether deformation is independent of species, followed by propagator/polarization and source conservation. A genuine gravity target needs gapless spin-2, universal conserved stress coupling, no long-range scalar/vector contamination, recovered Lorentz invariance and no wake drag. Do not calibrate Newton strength until these gates.

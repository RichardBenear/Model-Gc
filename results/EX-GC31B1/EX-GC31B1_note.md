## Method
T(a;b,c) has source a and unordered distinct targets b,c. Gate (x,y,z,u,v), all distinct, reads T(x;y,z), T(y;z,x), T(x;u,v), T(v;y,z); removes first three, retains fourth. Eight output triples from CSV use indices (y,z,u,v,w), w fresh. This matches the explicitly documented 55744 grammar including persistent guard. Existing identical triples are retained under chosen set semantics, not re-created; source-only deletion or other undocumented restrictions are not assumed. Every live relation has initial producer 0 or event producer ID. Dependency bitmask identifies whether the second event consumes or reads a relation produced by first. Rooted state comparison pins every initial vertex and allows the two fresh vertices to exchange, so it is stronger than unrooted graph isomorphism. Two-event DAG comparison checks read/consume dependency existence, not multiplicity or complete typed incidence; thus failure of combined gate is not evidence that bare DAG differs. Enumerate partial bijections of five roles, retain overlaps in consume/read or consume/consume; 179 configurations, including distinct matches with identical required relations when applicable. These are constructed legal seeds; no assertion of reachability from native vacuum. At each seed fire each designated competing match and enumerate every legal second event. Controls use disjoint matches in opposite order. No random seeds, no fitting, no global physical clock, no particle-protecting scheduler.

## Interpretation and next decision
The common gate architecture may be the constraint, not the output templates. Competing donor choices share consumed relations, so disjoint commutation cannot settle the question. Before additional physics runs, a cheap GC31B1 follow-up could use the original Stage W engine (local ZIP if provided) and test deeper causal completion on a small representative subset including 55744, 55584, 58846, and branching/nonbranching controls. Use exact producer/reader/consumer histories and canonical causal graph equivalence across equivalent cuts, rather than require same anchored live state after exactly two events. Test whether a witness is reachable in a native vacuum. If no meaningful qualification emerges, shift toward rule design by construction: local rewrites with provable conflict completion and no global particle selector. Only then use independent ball-growth and min-cut geometry measurements (StochasticVacuum control methodology) and two-mode operational propagation.

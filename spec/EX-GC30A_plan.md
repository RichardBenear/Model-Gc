# EX-GC30A — Common metric from vacuum structure (EXPLORATORY)
2026-11-22; design/run ChatGPT; PI go-ahead: "Go ahead with the test and incorporate any of it's suggestions if you like." Paper-only, default 2 core-hour cap, no production simulation. Prior GC28/29A closed, 29B deferred. Aim: distinguish emergent propagation geometry from assumed gravity.
Screen five arms: (A) multicomponent acoustic/condensate analogue, (B) shared microscopic kinetic tensor/collective tetrad (including fermionic quasiparticles), (C) Sakharov loop-induced metric, (D) entanglement/correlation geometry, (E) causal/relational graph geometry. Include explicit counterexamples, not only favorable versions. Read primary literature on Weinberg–Witten, analogue models, induced gravity, entanglement and causal geometry. 
Prospective criteria, scored P (demonstrated in specified reference model), F (counterexample/incompatible), U (not demonstrated); conditional P explicitly states assumptions. C1: specify microscopic variables and equations and identify which Weinberg–Witten hypothesis is absent or why theorem is not yet applicable; no claim exemption merely from naming an analogue. C2: derive hyperbolic Lorentzian characteristic cone from dynamics, not put a shared curved metric into matter action. C3: common cone for at least two distinct probe sectors including off-diagonal perturbations; common background speed alone insufficient; normalize kinetic tensors, require proportionality under open symmetry-preserving parameter/background variations, no individual retuning. C4: common full metric rather than cone only (mass/clock normalization and massive dispersion); if not tested U. C5: plausible independent energy-sourcing channel (localized energy, not species charge); distinguish implemented result from proposed test. C6: credible route to dynamical massless spin-2 with universal stress coupling and no extra long-range force or preferred-frame artifacts; no claim from kinematics alone. 
Selection rule: GC30B candidate must pass C1/C2 and have a concrete falsifiable multi-probe C3 test, even if C3 fails in generic control; prioritize testability, not number of speculative claims. No candidate advances as established gravity unless all criteria met. Show analytic two-sector principal symbol, symmetry control and minimal perturbation breaking universality; propose next test with independent observables and stop conditions. No GC30B production authorized here. Preserve prior negative results. Output note/source citations, review disposition, handoff and log.

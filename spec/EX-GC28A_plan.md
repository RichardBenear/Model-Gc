# EX-GC28A — exploratory vacuum fluctuation spectrum (registered scope)

Goal: establish a reliable finite-lattice quantum harmonic calculation and discriminate fixed-cutoff artifacts from a candidate physical energy response.

1. Full 1+1D phi4 kink benchmark: E=∫[phi_x²/2+mu²(phi²−1)²/2] dx; phi=tanh(mu x), vacuum fluctuation mass 2mu. Dirichlet endpoints, discrete odd constrained stationary kink (center fixed), Hessian on full interior; identify translation by overlap with derivative, set its frequency to zero as collective coordinate only if |lambda0|<.02 mu² and overlap>.99. Other negative modes block. Vacuum spectrum same grid. h=.2,.1,.05,.025, half-box 12 and 20 (mu=1); eta=±.02 with mu=exp(eta) at h=.1,.05, R=20. Raw vacuum difference and first-order trace subtraction: Zraw=.5 Tr(sqrt(Dk)-sqrt(Dv)), Born=.25 Tr(Dv^(-1/2)(Dk-Dv)), Zsub=Zraw−Born (hbar=1). This is the no-tadpole scheme in 1D; exact continuum Zsub=mu*(1/(2sqrt(3))−3/pi). Check discrete vacuum eigenvalues, shape mode omega²≈3mu², stable spectra, Zsub convergence and derivative vs exact (not precision gate).
2. 3D Skyrme B=1 *spherically symmetric radial fluctuation sector only*. No claim about full 3D spectrum, translational/isospin modes absent from the radial subspace. Discretize radial energy E/(12π²)=(1/3π)∫[(A r²+2B sin²f) f_r²+2A sin²f+B sin⁴f/r²+C r²(1−cos f)]dr, midpoint quadrature. Relax with analytic gradient, derive tridiagonal Hessian from same energy; check directional Hessian and generalized eigenvalue against dense reference. h=.2,.1,.05,.025; R=12,24; configurations C=0 and C=.17839644285107545 (GC27B coefficient). eta=±.02 at h=.1,.05 R=24, two couplings per C: scale-consistent (a,b,c)=(2,0,4), independent (2,0,0), A=e^(a eta), B=e^(b eta), C=C0 e^(c eta). Track classical energy, virial, raw/first-order-subtracted spectral energy, low modes and derivative dZ/deta, cutoff/box dependence. Born subtraction here is a diagnostic in chosen mass-normalized coordinates, not renormalization of full 3D Skyrme EFT. Note regulator and finite counterterm dependence. Also compare scale-consistent using comoving r grid h,R scaled exp(-eta), predicted E and frequencies exp(eta), to distinguish built-in symmetry.

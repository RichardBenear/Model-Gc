# EX-GC38B — is the entanglement temperature the clock? (lattice Bisognano–Wichmann and Tolman)

2026-11-26 | EXPLORATORY | Claude | PI go-ahead on decision card ("Yes, plan and run 38A then 38B", 2026-11-26 02:10 UTC). Written before any run. Default cap 2 core-hours; expected under 0.5. This is our construction, not LaViolette's Model G.

## Why

In the continuum, the vacuum restricted to a half-space is a thermal state of the boost generator (Bisognano–Wichmann). Its entanglement Hamiltonian is K = 2π ∫ x ℋ(x) dx, so the local temperature is 1/(2πx), Unruh's. In a static gravitational field the local temperature scales as 1/N (Tolman). If both hold on our lattice:
- the flow generated by K is a local time;
- its rate is set by the same lapse that sets the event rate in 35I/37B.

That would tie entanglement, thermodynamics, time and gravity together in one quantity.

## Method

- **Lattice:** a 1D chain, and the 3D lattice at fixed transverse momentum, with a Gaussian ground state. The entanglement Hamiltonian of the half-chain x > 0 is quadratic, K = ½(φ M φ + π N π). It is obtained from the X and P correlation matrices by the standard symplectic formulae.
- **Readouts:**
  - **R1:** the diagonal and nearest-neighbour coefficients of K against 2π·x·(local energy density coefficients), i.e. the lattice Bisognano–Wichmann profile;
  - **R2:** the effective local temperature β(x)⁻¹ from the ratio of K's gradient coefficient to the Hamiltonian's;
  - **R3, Tolman:** the same in the ground state of a chain whose couplings carry a static lapse N(x) (H = Σ N ℋ, as in 37A-S2). Does β(x) scale with N, i.e. T_local N = constant?
- **Masses:** m = 0.05–0.5, chain lengths 200–800.

## Predictions

- **P1 (scored):** away from the cut and from the far end, β(x)/(2πx) = 1 within 10% for x ≤ 20 at m = 0.05. This is the lattice Bisognano–Wichmann result; the literature reports approximate agreement on lattices.
- **P2 (scored):** with a smooth lapse N(x) = 1 + ε f(x), the change in local β follows the Tolman law within 10% of the change.
- **P3 (descriptive):** how far K departs from strictly local (range of non-local couplings).

## Checks

- **G1:** the entropy from K's spectrum equals the entropy from the symplectic eigenvalues.
- **G2:** a massive continuum limit compared with the analytic Rindler result.

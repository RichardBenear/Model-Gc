# Exact momentum and energy on a fixed lattice

2026-11-24 | Literature check accompanying [GC35J](../results/EX-GC35J/EX-GC35J_note.md).

Dmitriev, Kevrekidis and Yoshikawa show a trade-off between the standard momentum and energy invariants for their class of standard nearest-neighbor nonlinear Klein–Gordon discretizations. The competing schemes use discrete potential differences for momentum or a Hamiltonian force for energy. Kevrekidis, Putkaradze and Rapti report a related obstruction for generic data in their non-holonomic constraint construction. [Nearest-neighbor result](https://arxiv.org/abs/nlin/0506002) · [Non-holonomic formulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.05516)

The hypotheses concern particular degrees of freedom, stencils and definitions of the invariants. They do not establish a no-go theorem for every local nonlinear construction, for staggered Skyrme matter in general, or for a conserved-token rule. In Model Gc the direct reason for stopping this exact-Ward search is the explicit counterexample to the particular potential coupling in GC35J together with the stress failures in GC35H. Token-number conservation supplies an exact count in GC35L. It does not supply a continuum momentum tensor or causal-history equivalence.
